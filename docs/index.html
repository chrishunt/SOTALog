SOTA Log

Field-optimized QSO logger for SOTA & POTA activators

About

A fast, field-optimized QSO logger for SOTA and POTA activators — designed for one-handed use on cold summits and windy picnic tables.

Features

OmniField Entry Log callsign, RST, frequency, and references in a single text field
Live Spots See active SOTA and POTA spots; tap to prefill a QSO
SOTACat Integration Auto-sync frequency and mode with your radio; send CW macros with one tap
QRZ Sync Look up operator names, QTH, and profile photos from QRZ.com
ADIF Export Export logs in standard ADIF format for upload to any logging platform
Offline-First Everything works without internet — all data stored locally on device

Privacy Policy

SOTA Log contains no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no servers. All data is stored locally on your device.

  • No data collection or third-party sharing. We collect nothing and share nothing.
  • Optional QRZ sync. If you enter QRZ.com credentials, they are stored on-device and sent only to QRZ.com.
  • Location. Used only to calculate your Maidenhead grid square; never transmitted off-device.
  • ADIF export. Exported files are handled via the iOS share sheet — the app does not upload them.
  • Data retention. Your data persists until you delete it. Uninstalling removes all stored data.

System Requirements

iOS 17.0 or later. iPhone.
