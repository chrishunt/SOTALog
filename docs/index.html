SOTA Log

Log a QSO in seconds. SOTA & POTA field logger.

CW macros, SOTACat radio control, fully offline.

Features

One field. Whole QSO. Callsign, RST, frequency, references, space-separated in a single line.
Live spots & self-spotting SOTA & POTA spots in real time. Self-spot via SOTAmat SMS with no data.
SOTACat radio control Auto-sync frequency and mode. One-tap CW macros.
QRZ sync Upload QSOs, download your logbook, and look up callsigns from QRZ.com.
ADIF export Standard format. Upload to any logging platform.
Works offline Everything functions without internet. All data stays on device.

Privacy

No analytics. No ads. No tracking. No servers.

Read full privacy policy
  • No data collection or third-party sharing. We collect nothing and share nothing.
  • Optional QRZ sync. If you enter QRZ.com credentials, they are stored on-device and sent only to QRZ.com.
  • Location. Used only to calculate your Maidenhead grid square; never transmitted off-device.
  • SOTACat. Communicates with your SOTACat device over local Wi-Fi. No data leaves your local network.
  • ADIF export. Exported files are handled via the iOS share sheet. The app does not upload them.
  • Data retention. Your data persists until you delete it. Uninstalling removes all stored data.
